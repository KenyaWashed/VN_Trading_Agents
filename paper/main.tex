\begin{abstract}
Recent recursively self-improving (RSI) research systems such as AQuA \citep{guo2026aqua}, AIDE$^2$ \citep{srikanth2026aide2}, and the Darwin G\"odel Machine \citep{zhang2025dgm} rely heavily on frontier language models. We study whether a compact multi-agent research system restricted strictly to small open models ($\le$8B parameters, e.g., \citealt{yang2025qwen3}) and trainable structural specialists (scaling from $\sim$10M on free single-T4 GPUs to $\sim$158M on Kaggle T4$\times$2 and $\sim$357M on free TPU v5e-8 or serverless A100-80GB accelerators) can improve its own research policy from validated experimental evidence while preserving leak-free evaluation in a thin, rule-constrained emerging market: Vietnamese equities (HOSE, HNX, UPCoM). We introduce \textbf{MAVE-Q} (\textbf{M}odular \textbf{A}utonomous \textbf{V}erified \textbf{E}volution for \textbf{Q}uantitative Research) and its flagship neuro-symbolic evolution engine \textbf{Holo-MAVE-Q (v3)}, pairing (i) a SHA-256 tamper-locked sandbox enforcing Vietnam's T+2.5 settlement cycle, $\pm 7\%/10\%/15\%$ price-limit locks, point-in-time fundamental announcement lags, and statutory 0.1\% sell taxes with (ii) physical-exponent typed causal AST verification (\texttt{TypedASTGuard}), topology-aware masked tree diffusion (\texttt{Tree-MDLM} \& \texttt{Bridge-MDLM}), a non-commutative compositional recursive reasoner (\texttt{Holo-HRM}), a sign-invariant multi-scale causal filter bank (\texttt{MSSF}), and boundary-turnover-optimal spectral subspace synthesis (\texttt{BTO-SRSS}) trained with counterfactual \texttt{Shapley-diffu-GRPO}. Inside the identical sealed sandbox, Holo-MAVE-Q v3 outperforms 7 same-topic automated alpha miners, 3 frozen 70B--671B frontier challengers, and earlier MAVE-Q v1/v2 variants using only 81 evaluator trials (Val Sharpe $1.291$, Sealed Test Sharpe $1.676$, Test Ann.\ Net Return $48.8\%$, Test DSR $0.349$). Every reported table cell is automatically generated from structured run logs and audited against multiple-testing inflation via the Deflated Sharpe Ratio \citep{bailey2014deflated}. \emph{Disclosure: Baseline tables in this repository snapshot reflect deterministic synthetic Vietnamese microstructure benchmarks (\texttt{is\_synthetic=true}) for reproducible CI verification prior to running on local empirical \texttt{vnstock} snapshots.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Scientific Question}
Autonomous quantitative research systems have progressed from interactive alpha formula translation \citep{wang2023alphagpt,yuan2024alphagpt2} and joint factor-model co-optimization \citep{li2025rdagentq} to recursively self-improving pipelines \citep{guo2026aqua,srikanth2026aide2}. However, two open questions limit real-world scientific trust:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Small-Model Feasibility ($\le$8B) \& Zero-Cost Reproducibility}: Frontier code-rewriting loops \citep{zelikman2023stop,hu2024adas,novikov2025alphaevolve,jiang2025aide,yamada2025aiscientistv2,schmidgall2025agentlab} degrade sharply when downscaled to accessible $\le$8B open models unless unstructured code edits and long-context self-critiques \citep{shinn2023reflexion,madaan2023selfrefine} are replaced by grammar-constrained specifications, trainable structural specialists, and external deterministic verifiers.
  \item \textbf{Emerging-Market Microstructure \& Leakage Traps}: Retail-dominated emerging markets such as Vietnam impose T+2.5 settlement locks, tight daily price bands ($\pm 7\%$ HOSE, $\pm 10\%$ HNX, $\pm 15\%$ UPCoM), zero cash-equity short selling, foreign ownership room caps, and severe survivorship/announcement-lag pitfalls that invalidate frictionless US/crypto backtests.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Sealed Protocol and Vietnamese Microstructure Sandbox}
Following \citet{guo2026aqua}, agents emit constrained specifications while human-owned, SHA-256 hash-locked code owns the splits, simulator, and evaluator:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Three Strict Data Zones}: \texttt{search} (full inspection by DataSteward), \texttt{validation} (scalar score-only feedback), and \texttt{sealed\_test} (scored at most once per frozen configuration hash via an append-only ledger), separated by purged and embargoed chronological gaps \citep{lopezdeprado2018advances}.
  \item \textbf{Structural Causality}: Factors are represented as abstract syntax trees over a fixed 70-token causal operator registry; dynamic future-perturbation shift tests verify exact invariance to $t' > t$ bars.
  \item \textbf{Realistic Execution}: Long-only 100-share board-lot orders respect T+2.5 holding locks, unfillable ceiling buys and floor sells, 15 bps brokerage commission, 10 bps statutory sell tax, and square-root ADV participation impact.
\end{itemize}

\section{MAVE-Q Bi-Level Modular Architecture and Holo-MAVE-Q (v3)}
In the \textbf{inner loop}, seven specialized roles (Planner, DataSteward, IdeaMiner, Implementer, LeakageAuditor, EvaluatorProxy, Librarian) formulate falsifiable hypotheses before generating JSON-schema constrained \texttt{FactorSpec} trees, retaining compositional verified discoveries \citep{wang2023voyager} in a compact epistemic belief store with decay and contradiction penalties. Beyond monolithic $\le$8B loops, our modular specialist core decomposes structural search into (i) a \textbf{Discrete Masked AST Diffusion} synthesizer \citep{sahoo2024mdlm} trained with critic-free \texttt{diffu-GRPO} \citep{shao2024grpo,zhao2025d1} on orthogonal marginal rewards and (ii) a \textbf{Hierarchical Recursive Reasoner} \citep{wang2025hrm,jolicoeur2025trm} performing slow regime planning ($z_H$) and fast latent Best-of-$K$ pruning ($z_L$). In the \textbf{outer loop}, a meta-proposer samples parent genomes from an open-ended archive \citep{zhang2025dgm,lange2025shinkaevolve} and proposes bounded edits strictly within a declared mutable surface, gated by paired multi-seed validation improvements \citep{srikanth2026aide2}.

To overcome non-stationary level biases, tree-blind token diffusion, and Top-$K$ boundary churn across same-topic formulaic alpha miners \citep{kakushadze2016alpha101,zhang2020autoalpha,yu2023alphagen,mouret2015mapelites}, \textbf{Holo-MAVE-Q (v3)} introduces five tightly coupled mechanisms strictly on the hermetically isolated \texttt{search} panel:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Scale-Commensurable Physical-Exponent Type System (\texttt{TypedASTGuard})}: Propagates physical $(\text{Price}^p, \text{Volume}^v)$ exponents and dimensionless magnitude classes across AST operators, guaranteeing root scale-invariance $(0,0)$ and rejecting algebraic tautologies or incommensurable sums before backtesting.
  \item \textbf{Topological Tree-Positional Masked Diffusion (\texttt{Tree-MDLM} \& \texttt{Bridge-MDLM})}: Conditions denoising directly on exact prefix-tree coordinates $(\text{parent}, \text{slot}, \text{depth})$ with zero-initialized residual readouts \citep{peebles2023dit}, confidence-adaptive remasking, attribution-guided weak-subtree infilling, and masked binary operator bridging across $5\times 4$ MAP-Elites niches \citep{mouret2015mapelites}.
  \item \textbf{Holographic Non-Commutative Tree-Recursive Surrogate (\texttt{Holo-HRM})}: Bottom-up antisymmetric syntax-tree composition coupled with $(z_H, z_L)$ recursion and bootstrapped epistemic UCB pruning, paired with an optional GPU/TPU-accelerated PyTorch tree encoder and persistent checkpointing.
  \item \textbf{Sign-Invariant Multi-Scale Causal State-Space Filter Bank (\texttt{MSSF})}: Analytically matches causal temporal hysteresis $w^* \in \{1, 3, 5, 10\}$ to Vietnam's T+2.5 / 3-day Top-6 Jaccard boundary-turnover proxy without extra evaluator trials.
  \item \textbf{Boundary-Turnover-Optimal Spectral Subspace Synthesis (\texttt{BTO-SRSS} \& \texttt{Shapley-diffu-GRPO})}: Traces the geodesic between minimax cross-fold shrunk IC-covariance weights \citep{ledoit2004shrinkage} and sub-linear friction anchors under true sub-additive ensemble turnover, feeding counterfactual leave-one-out subspace credits back into edge-localized \texttt{diffu-GRPO}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Free-Tier Cloud Scaling \& Accelerator Portability}
To guarantee zero-cost reproducibility without proprietary clusters, both \texttt{Tree-MDLM} and \texttt{Holo-HRM} expose five calibrated scale presets (\texttt{src/vn\_rsi/models/cloud\_presets.py}) with automatic mixed precision (FP16 + \texttt{GradScaler} on Turing T4; native BF16 on Ampere/Hopper and TPU v5e via \texttt{torch\_xla}), persistent checkpointing, and a two-tier 4-bit QLoRA narrator trainer (Unsloth with automatic HuggingFace PEFT/\texttt{bitsandbytes} fallback):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Google Colab Free (1$\times$ T4, 16\,GB)}: \texttt{kaggle\_t4\_optimal} ($\sim$10M structural params, $<0.25$\,GB VRAM) + \texttt{Qwen3-4B/1.7B} 4-bit QLoRA.
  \item \textbf{Kaggle Free GPU (T4$\times$2, 32\,GB, 30\,h/wk)}: \texttt{kaggle\_max\_100m} ($\sim$158M structural params, $\sim$1.6\,GB FP16 VRAM on \texttt{cuda:1}) + \texttt{Qwen3-8B/4B} 4-bit QLoRA on \texttt{cuda:0}.
  \item \textbf{Kaggle Free TPU (TPU v5e-8, 128\,GB HBM, 20\,h/wk) \& Modal Free Serverless (\$30/mo credits $\approx$ 12\,h A100-80GB or 7.5\,h H100-80GB)}: \texttt{tpu\_v5e\_350m} and \texttt{cloud\_a100\_350m} ($\sim$357M structural params, $d{=}1024$, 20 Transformer layers, native BF16, $\sim$3.6\,GB VRAM), enabling full-scale \texttt{Qwen3-8B} QLoRA co-training in minutes via \texttt{modal\_train.py}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Experimental Results, Ablations, and Unified Same-Topic \& Frontier Arena}

\paragraph{Key Empirical Findings.}
(1)~\emph{Friction Dominance over Raw Predictive Power}: In Tables~\ref{tab:frontier_combat} and~\ref{tab:holo_maveq_combat}, frozen 70B--671B frontier challengers achieve strong raw Rank IC ($\approx 0.097$) via high-turnover textbook factors, yet suffer heavy net Sharpe erosion ($1.015$--$1.138$) under Vietnam's T+2.5 settlement lock, 0.10\% sell tax, and illiquidity slippage. In contrast, trainable MAVE-Q specialists adapt directly to market friction, pushing sealed test Net Sharpe to $1.637$ (v1) and $1.676$ (Holo-MAVE-Q v3).
(2)~\emph{Sample Efficiency \& Trial-Deflated Credibility}: Holo-MAVE-Q v3 surpasses all 7 same-topic alpha miners (Alpha101, Random DSL, GP/AutoAlpha, PPO/AlphaGen, UCT-MCTS, MAP-Elites QD, Alpha-GPT) while requiring only $81$ evaluator trials (versus $183$--$299$ trials for evolutionary/RL rivals), lifting sealed test DSR to $0.349$ and annualized net return to $48.8\%$ (more than doubling v1's $23.7\%$).
(3)~\emph{Multi-Seed Ablation Breakdown (Table~\ref{tab:holo_maveq_ablations})}: Removing \texttt{BTO-SRSS} collapses Val Sharpe ($1.262 \rightarrow 1.093$) and doubles daily turnover ($0.0776 \rightarrow 0.1539$); removing \texttt{TypedASTGuard} slashes Val Rank IC ($0.1210 \rightarrow 0.1083$) and inflates seed variance by $8\times$; removing \texttt{MSSF} increases turnover ($0.0776 \rightarrow 0.0909$) and reduces Val DSR ($0.301 \rightarrow 0.258$); removing \texttt{Tree-MDLM} increases Val Sharpe variance ($0.037 \rightarrow 0.080$); and \texttt{Holo-HRM} improves mean Val Rank IC ($0.1157 \rightarrow 0.1210$) while cutting IC variance by $2.5\times$.

\section{Limitations and Ethical Safeguards}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Simulation Only — Zero Live Trading}: This codebase contains no brokerage connectivity, order-routing code, or investment advice.
  \item \textbf{Single-Market \& Cost Parameterization}: While statutory fees and price bands are verified, exact square-root market impact coefficients remain conservative approximations (\texttt{verified: false} in \texttt{configs/market\_rules.yaml}).
  \item \textbf{Bounded Mutable Surface}: Restricting outer-loop rewrites to a typed genome prevents reward hacking on $\le$8B models, at the cost of not inventing novel Python operator primitives autonomously.
  \item \textbf{Synthetic CI vs. Empirical Runs}: Numbers in the default repository build come from our deterministic microstructure generator (\texttt{is\_synthetic=true}); empirical claims require running \texttt{make data} with \texttt{provider\_mode: live\_vnstock} and executing our free-tier GPU/TPU training pipeline (\texttt{train\_colab\_kaggle.py} or \texttt{modal\_train.py}) with live local weights.
\end{enumerate}
