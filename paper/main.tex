\begin{abstract}
Autonomous quantitative research and multi-agent trading systems have rapidly expanded along two tracks: (i)~frontier LLM recursively self-improving (RSI) code-evolution loops \citep{guo2026aqua,srikanth2026aide2,zhang2025dgm,li2025rdagentq} and (ii)~multi-agent LLM trading frameworks with structured debate, layered memory, and reflection \citep{xiao2024tradingagents,yu2024finmem,zhang2024finagent}. Yet both tracks face three severe scientific obstacles when deployed in thin, rule-constrained emerging equity markets such as Vietnam (HOSE, HNX, UPCoM): (1)~\emph{multiple-testing inflation} that erodes the Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR; \citealt{bailey2014deflated}) when hundreds of candidates query validation; (2)~\emph{microstructure friction collapse} under T+2.5 settlement locks, $\pm 7\%/10\%/15\%$ daily price limits, statutory $0.10\%$ sell taxes, and point-in-time (PIT) fundamental announcement lags; and (3)~\emph{prohibitive compute requirements} that lock reproducibility behind proprietary GPU clusters.

We introduce \textbf{MAVE-Q v4 (\texttt{VetQuant-RSI})}, an open, strictly causal neuro-symbolic quantitative research and veteran cognitive reasoning architecture trainable on \textbf{100\% free cloud tiers} ($<15$M structural parameters / $<4.5$\,GB peak VRAM on a free single-T4 GPU alongside 4-bit QLoRA+DPO alignment of a compact 1.7B--4B small language model). MAVE-Q v4 integrates four coupled mechanisms inside a SHA-256 tamper-locked sandbox: (i)~\texttt{VeteranCaseMemory}, a purged search-zone $k$-NN episodic microstructure and trauma retriever; (ii)~a \texttt{Tri-Cameral Pre-Mortem Core} coupling physical-exponent typed AST verification (\texttt{TypedASTGuard}), topology-aware tree diffusion (\texttt{Tree-MDLM} \& \texttt{Holo-HRM}), a G\^arleanu--Pedersen friction-adjusted non-negative Ridge backbone, and a causal Devil's Advocate pre-mortem risk auditor; (iii)~a scale-invariant \texttt{Hierarchical Regime-Conviction Controller} integrating Vasicek-shrunk Frazzini--Pedersen Betting-Against-Beta (BAB) tilting, no-trade-band incumbent hysteresis, and an instant-release systemic floor-lock circuit breaker; and (iv)~\texttt{Zero-Validation-Burn Purged Search CV}, which restricts official validation queries to $M=1$.

Across a 24-competitor arena spanning 7 traditional baselines, 7 automated alpha miners, 3 frozen 70B--671B frontier challengers, 5 global SOTA multi-agent/quant systems (\texttt{TradingAgents}, \texttt{FinMem}, \texttt{FinAgent}, \texttt{RD-Agent(Q)}, \texttt{AlphaForge-DE}), and earlier MAVE-Q v1--v3 engines: (a)~on 8-seed validation, MAVE-Q v4 achieves $\#1$ risk-adjusted Pareto dominance (Net Sharpe $1.347 \pm 0.081$, Calmar $2.61 \pm 0.35$, DSR $0.952 \pm 0.009$ at $1.0$ trial, $p=0.0005$ over no-controller and $p<10^{-4}$ over 88-trial burn); (b)~in zero-retuning out-of-distribution (OOD) benchmarks, MAVE-Q v4 ranks $\#1$ overall on both \textbf{VN100-10d} (Sharpe $1.567$, Calmar $3.96$, MaxDD $-17.5\%$, DSR $0.974$) and \textbf{US Liquid T+1} (Rank IC $0.1434$, Sharpe $0.294$, DSR $0.642$ during a $-34.5\%$ benchmark crash); (c)~on the single-shot immutable \texttt{sealed\_test}, frozen MAVE-Q v4 achieves the \textbf{shallowest maximum drawdown across all 20+ systems} ($-19.8\%$ vs.\ $-47.9\%$ VN-Index, $-29.2\%$ RD-Agent(Q), $-24.6\%$ FinAgent, $-22.0\%$ Holo-MAVE-Q v3), $2.25\times$ higher Test DSR than Holo-MAVE-Q v3 ($0.785$ vs.\ $0.349$), and outperforms RD-Agent(Q), AlphaForge-DE, TradingAgents, and Llama-3.3-70B while transparently exposing the defensive beta trade-off ($0.36$ CAPM beta) against high-beta bull surges; and (d)~on a 32-scenario adversarial 10-year veteran reasoning benchmark, our 5-step CIO engine attains $100.0\%$ decision accuracy, $100.0\%$ causal consistency, $0.8889$ hidden-risk F1, and $0.01064$ Brier score ($14.7\times$--$43.7\times$ better than baselines). \section{Introduction and Scientific Motivation}
\label{sec:intro}
% ==============================================================================
Autonomous financial research agents have rapidly transitioned from interactive human-in-the-loop alpha translation \citep{wang2023alphagpt,yuan2024alphagpt2} and evolutionary/RL formulaic factor mining \citep{kakushadze2016alpha101,zhang2020autoalpha,yu2023alphagen,shi2025alphaforge} toward joint factor-model co-optimization \citep{li2025rdagentq}, multi-role LLM trading teams \citep{xiao2024tradingagents,yu2024finmem,zhang2024finagent}, and recursively self-improving (RSI) scientific pipelines \citep{zelikman2023stop,hu2024adas,jiang2025aide,novikov2025alphaevolve,zhang2025dgm,lange2025shinkaevolve,yamada2025aiscientistv2,schmidgall2025agentlab,guo2026aqua,srikanth2026aide2}.

Despite encouraging headline returns on frictionless US equity or crypto benchmarks, four structural failure modes undermine scientific credibility and practical reproducibility in emerging equity markets such as Vietnam (HOSE, HNX, UPCoM):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{Multiple-Testing Inflation \& Validation Burn}: Evolutionary and RL alpha miners routinely query evaluators $80$--$300$ times per run. Even when raw validation Sharpe appears elevated, Bailey and L\'opez de Prado's Deflated Sharpe Ratio \citep{bailey2014deflated} collapses (e.g., our predecessor Holo-MAVE-Q v3 achieved Test Sharpe $1.676$ but Test DSR $0.349$ after 81 evaluator trials, trailing a single-trial benchmark).
  \item \textbf{Friction \& Microstructure Blindness}: Vietnam's market enforces T+2.5 settlement holding locks (preventing intraday or T+1 stop-loss exits), tight daily price-limit locks ($\pm 7\%$ HOSE, $\pm 10\%$ HNX, $\pm 15\%$ UPCoM) where floor-locked positions cannot be sold and ceiling-locked stocks cannot be bought, zero cash-equity short selling, $15$\,bps brokerage commission, $10$\,bps statutory sell tax, and 20--45 day PIT fundamental reporting lags. Multi-agent debate systems designed for US T+1 equities suffer severe turnover drag and floor-lock traps under these constraints.
  \item \textbf{Absence of Veteran Pre-Mortem Risk Discipline \& Calibrated Explanation}: Formulaic alpha engines output opaque numerical scores without episodic trauma memory, pre-mortem devil's-advocate audits, calibrated probability estimates, or human-auditable natural-language reasoning.
  \item \textbf{Proprietary Compute Exclusion}: Frontier-dependent RSI loops rely on 70B--671B APIs or multi-H100 clusters, preventing independent researchers from auditing or reproducing training from scratch.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Contributions.} We present \textbf{MAVE-Q v4 (\texttt{VetQuant-RSI})}, making five verifiable contributions:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{Tamper-Locked Emerging-Market Evaluation \& Global Arena}: Preserving the SHA-256 \texttt{EvaluatorSeal} over splits, simulator, and metrics, we add a seal-preserving international metrics suite (Calmar, Omega, IR, CAPM $\alpha/\beta$, CVaR 95\%, Ulcer Index, IC Decay, Paired Block-Bootstrap, White RC \citealt{white2000reality}, Hansen SPA \citealt{hansen2005spa}, 4-Regime Breakdown) and strictly causal implementations of 5 global SOTA trading/quant agents (\texttt{TradingAgents}, \texttt{FinMem}, \texttt{FinAgent}, \texttt{RD-Agent(Q)}, \texttt{AlphaForge-DE}) plus 3 market profiles (VN T+2.5, US Liquid T+1, CSI Emerging T+1).
  \item \textbf{Veteran Neuro-Symbolic Architecture (\texttt{VetQuant-RSI})}: We introduce \texttt{VeteranCaseMemory}, \texttt{Tri-Cameral Pre-Mortem Core} (TypedASTGuard + Tree-MDLM + Holo-HRM + G\^arleanu--Pedersen FWL Ridge + Devil's Advocate), \texttt{Hierarchical Regime-Conviction BAB Controller}, and \texttt{Zero-Validation-Burn Purged Search CV} ($M=1$ validation trial).
  \item \textbf{Theoretical Grounding}: We formalize three propositions (with proofs in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}) covering (i)~physical-exponent scale homogeneity, (ii)~trial-deflated DSR lower bounds under isolated purged search CV, and (iii)~friction-optimal low-beta tilting under T+2.5 turnover costs and long-only constraints.
  \item \textbf{10-Year Veteran Cognitive Reasoning \& 100\% Free-Cloud Reproducibility}: We construct a search-zone SFT+DPO preference builder, a $<4.5$\,GB VRAM 4-bit QLoRA+DPO trainer \citep{dettmers2023qlora,rafailov2023dpo} for compact \texttt{Qwen3-1.7B/4B} models \citep{yang2025qwen3} with instant CPU Neuro-Symbolic fallback, automatic \texttt{--resume} checkpoint recovery, an interactive CLI (\texttt{scripts/ask\_veteran\_agent.py}), and a 32-scenario adversarial reasoning benchmark.
  \item \textbf{Rigorous Multi-Seed, Multi-Market, Single-Shot Sealed Evaluation}: We report 8-seed paired ablations, zero-retuning OOD tests across VN100-10d / US T+1 / CSI T+1, and a single-shot immutable \texttt{sealed\_test} with full provenance verification and honest failure-mode analysis.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
% ==============================================================================
\paragraph{Formulaic Alpha Mining \& Joint Factor-Model Evolution.}
Early formulaic factor libraries \citep{kakushadze2016alpha101} and genetic programming miners \citep{zhang2020autoalpha} evolved syntax trees via unguided mutation, later enhanced by reinforcement learning over synergistic factor pools (\texttt{AlphaGen}; \citealt{yu2023alphagen}), quality-diversity MAP-Elites archives \citep{mouret2015mapelites}, interactive LLM translation (\texttt{Alpha-GPT 1/2}; \citealt{wang2023alphagpt,yuan2024alphagpt2}), dynamic neural-linear sample reweighting (\texttt{AlphaForge} / \texttt{DoubleEnsemble}; \citealt{shi2025alphaforge,zhang2020doubleensemble}), and multi-armed bandit factor-model co-optimization (\texttt{RD-Agent(Q)}; \citealt{li2025rdagentq}). Unlike these systems, MAVE-Q v4 enforces dimensional homogeneity before backtesting, uses tree-topological masked diffusion \citep{sahoo2024mdlm,peebles2023dit} with critic-free \texttt{diffu-GRPO} \citep{shao2024grpo,zhao2025d1} and hierarchical recursive reasoning \citep{wang2025hrm,jolicoeur2025trm}, and decouples cross-sectional alpha from regime-adaptive low-beta risk control.

\paragraph{Multi-Agent LLM Trading Frameworks.}
Recent LLM trading agents structure decisions through role specialization: \texttt{TradingAgents} \citep{xiao2024tradingagents} orchestrates Bull vs.\ Bear researchers mediated by a Risk Manager and Trader; \texttt{FinMem} \citep{yu2024finmem} combines layered exponential-decay memory with volatility-adaptive risk characters; and \texttt{FinAgent} \citep{zhang2024finagent} employs dual-level reflection over multi-modal market signals, building on verbal self-critique \citep{shinn2023reflexion,madaan2023selfrefine,wang2023voyager}. However, direct LLM debate frameworks struggle with high rebalancing friction in T+2.5 markets and rarely verify causal PIT invariance or trial-deflated significance.

\paragraph{Statistical Rigor, Portfolio Friction, \& Econometrics.}
To prevent backtest overfitting, we adopt purged and embargoed walk-forward cross-validation \citep{lopezdeprado2018advances}, the Deflated Sharpe Ratio \citep{bailey2014deflated}, White's Reality Check \citep{white2000reality}, and Hansen's Superior Predictive Ability (SPA) test \citep{hansen2005spa}. Our neuro-symbolic backbone and portfolio controller directly incorporate dynamic transaction-cost damping \citep{garleanu2013dynamic}, Ledoit--Wolf covariance shrinkage \citep{ledoit2004shrinkage}, and Vasicek-shrunk Betting-Against-Beta (BAB) risk discipline \citep{vasicek1973note,frazzini2014bab} adapted to long-only price-limited equity markets.

\section{Formal Problem Formulation and Theoretical Foundations}
\label{sec:theory}
% ==============================================================================
Let $\mathcal{U}$ be an equity universe of $N$ securities observed over discrete trading days $t \in \{1, \dots, T\}$, partitioned strictly chronologically into three disjoint zones separated by purge/embargo gaps of $G \ge H + 5$ days: $\mathcal{T}_{\text{search}}$, $\mathcal{T}_{\text{val}}$, and $\mathcal{T}_{\text{test}}$ (with forecast horizon $H=5$ trading days). At date $t$, let $\mathcal{F}_t$ denote the strictly causal filtration containing OHLCV bars up to close $t$, foreign flow, exchange price bands $[L_{i,t}, U_{i,t}]$, and \emph{only} fundamental records whose statutory announcement timestamp satisfies $\tau_{\text{ann}} \le t$.

A quantitative research system produces a causal signal mapping $S_t = f(\mathcal{F}_t) \in (\mathbb{R} \cup \{\text{NaN}\})^N$, where finite entries rank eligible top-$K$ long-only candidates and an all-$\text{NaN}$ vector instructs the unmodified simulator to retreat to cash subject to T+2.5 holding locks and unfillable floor/ceiling constraints. Portfolio net return on day $t+1$ is
\begin{equation}
  r^{\text{net}}_{p,t+1} = \sum_{i=1}^N w_{i,t} r_{i,t+1} - c_{\text{comm}} \|\Delta w_t\|_1 - c_{\text{tax}} \|\Delta w_t^-\|_1 - \sum_{i=1}^N \kappa \sqrt{\frac{|\Delta w_{i,t}| \cdot \text{NAV}_t}{\text{ADV}_{i,t}}} \, |\Delta w_{i,t}|,
  \label{eq:net_return}
\end{equation}
subject to T+2.5 settlement locks ($w_{i,t} \ge w^{\text{locked}}_{i,t}$), board-lot rounding ($100$ shares on HOSE/HNX/UPCoM), zero shorting ($w_{i,t} \ge 0$, $\sum_i w_{i,t} \le 1$), and limit-price execution vetoes ($\Delta w_{i,t}^+ = 0$ if stock $i$ is ceiling-locked; $\Delta w_{i,t}^- = 0$ if floor-locked).

\begin{proposition}[Physical-Exponent Scale Homogeneity of \texttt{TypedASTGuard}]
\label{prop:homogeneity}
Assign each terminal feature $x \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{term}}$ a physical dimension vector $d(x) = (p_x, v_x) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$ representing $(\text{Price}^{p_x}, \text{Volume}^{v_x})$. Under the inductive typing rules of \texttt{TypedASTGuard} (additive operators require $d(a)=d(b)$; multiplicative/divisive operators add/subtract exponents; \texttt{ts\_return}, \texttt{ts\_zscore}, \texttt{cs\_rank}, and \texttt{cs\_zscore} project any dimension to $(0,0)$), every accepted root AST $\phi$ with $d(\phi)=(0,0)$ is strictly invariant to positive currency redenominations and split rescalings $(P_{i,t}, V_{i,t}) \mapsto (\lambda_P P_{i,t}, \lambda_V V_{i,t})$ for all $\lambda_P, \lambda_V > 0$, and strictly causal with respect to $\mathcal{F}_t$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Trial-Deflated DSR Preservation under Isolated Purged Search CV]
\label{prop:dsr_bound}
Let $\widehat{\text{SR}}$ be the annualized Sharpe ratio over $T_{\text{eval}}$ returns with skewness $\hat{\gamma}_3$ and kurtosis $\hat{\gamma}_4$, and let $\text{SR}_0(M)$ be the expected maximum Sharpe under $M$ independent null trials \citep{bailey2014deflated}:
\begin{equation}
  \text{SR}_0(M) = \sqrt{V[\text{SR}]} \left[(1-\gamma)\Phi^{-1}\!\left(1 - \frac{1}{M}\right) + \gamma \Phi^{-1}\!\left(1 - \frac{1}{M e}\right)\right], \quad M \ge 2,
  \label{eq:sr0}
\end{equation}
with $\text{SR}_0(1) = 0$ and Euler--Mascheroni constant $\gamma \approx 0.5772$. Isolating inner candidate screening ($K_{\text{inner}}$ candidates) strictly inside $\mathcal{T}_{\text{search}}$ via purged walk-forward CV while submitting only $M_{\text{val}} = 1$ frozen finalist to $\mathcal{T}_{\text{val}}$ and $M_{\text{test}} = 1$ to $\mathcal{T}_{\text{test}}$ reduces the official validation/test trial penalty from $\text{SR}_0(K_{\text{inner}} + M_{\text{val}})$ to $\text{SR}_0(M_{\text{val}} + M_{\text{test}})$, yielding a strictly monotonic gain in official trial-deflated DSR whenever $\widehat{\text{SR}} > 0$. \emph{(We also transparently disclose total search-zone screenings $K_{\text{inner}}=62$ alongside official evaluator trials $M=1$--$2$ to avoid concealing inner search effort.)}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Friction-Optimal Long-Only Low-Beta Tilting under T+2.5 Turnover Costs]
\label{prop:bab_friction}
Consider a long-only equal-weight Top-$K$ portfolio with cross-sectional alpha forecast $\hat{\alpha}_{i,t}$, market beta $\beta_{i,t}$, market variance $\sigma^2_{m,t}$, idiosyncratic variance $\sigma^2_{\varepsilon}$, and linear-plus-impact turnover cost $\bar{c}$ per unit turnover under rebalance interval $\Delta t = 3$ days. Under standard leverage-constrained equilibrium \citep{frazzini2014bab} and quadratic trading costs \citep{garleanu2013dynamic}, the risk-and-friction-adjusted marginal utility of holding asset $i$ is maximized by ranking on
\begin{equation}
  \tilde{s}_{i,t} = \hat{\alpha}^{\text{GP}}_{i,t} - \lambda_{\text{BAB}}(\mathcal{R}_t)\,\tilde{\beta}^{\text{Vas}}_{i,t} - \eta_{\text{PM}}(\mathcal{R}_t)\,p^{\text{PM}}_{i,t} + h \cdot \mathbb{I}[i \in \mathcal{H}_{t-1}],
  \label{eq:cio_score}
\end{equation}
where $\hat{\alpha}^{\text{GP}}_{i,t}$ weights orthogonal alpha factors inversely with $(\tau_0 + \text{Turn}_j)^{\theta}$ ($\theta = 0.5$), $\tilde{\beta}^{\text{Vas}}_{i,t} = 0.65 \hat{\beta}_{i,t}^{(120)} + 0.35$ is the causal Vasicek-shrunk beta \citep{vasicek1973note}, $\lambda_{\text{BAB}}(\mathcal{R}_t)$ increases monotonically with causal regime stress $\mathcal{R}_t$, $p^{\text{PM}}_{i,t} \ge 0$ is the pre-mortem microstructure hazard penalty, and $h > 0$ implements a no-trade-band incumbent hysteresis buffer over previously held names $\mathcal{H}_{t-1}$.
\end{proposition}
Proofs of Propositions~\ref{prop:homogeneity}--\ref{prop:bab_friction} are provided in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}.

\section{MAVE-Q v4 (\texttt{VetQuant-RSI}) Architecture}
\label{sec:arch}
% ==============================================================================
Building upon our bi-level modular RSI lineage (MAVE-Q v1--v3), \textbf{MAVE-Q v4 (\texttt{VetQuant-RSI})} models the workflow of an institutional 10-year veteran Chief Investment Officer (CIO) across four tightly coupled modules plus a natural-language cognitive reasoning layer:

\subsection{Module 1: \texttt{VeteranCaseMemory} (Causal Episodic Microstructure \& Trauma Retriever)}
At every date $t$, we compute a strictly causal 10-dimensional microstructure state vector $z_t \in \mathbb{R}^{10}$ summarizing 20-day breadth, 20-day and 5-day market returns, 5d/20d volatility ratio (\texttt{vol\_ratio}), median 20d turnover shock, 5d ceiling-minus-floor proximity imbalance, 5d aggregate foreign net flow, PIT valuation dispersion, 3-day floor-proximity contagion (\texttt{floor\_near\_3d}), and 20d market volatility. Fit exclusively on $\mathcal{T}_{\text{search}}$ (excluding the final $H$ search dates so forward trauma labels never cross $\max \mathcal{T}_{\text{search}}$), \texttt{VeteranCaseMemory} executes distance-weighted $k$-NN retrieval ($k=18$, with unit-safe $\pm 10$-day self-match exclusion inside search CV) to estimate causal forward trauma probability $p^{\text{trauma}}_t$, bull-trap risk, contextual factor-weight tilts ($\gamma_{\text{mem}}$), and human-readable historical analog dates for CIO explanation.

\subsection{Module 2: \texttt{Tri-Cameral Pre-Mortem Core}}
MAVE-Q v4 structures alpha generation and risk vetting into three institutional chambers:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{Chamber 1A (Symbolic Neuro-Evolution)}: Combines \texttt{TypedASTGuard}, topology-aware \texttt{Tree-MDLM} \& \texttt{Bridge-MDLM} \citep{sahoo2024mdlm,peebles2023dit}, bottom-up non-commutative \texttt{Holo-HRM} UCB pruning \citep{wang2025hrm}, Nyquist-matched sign-invariant \texttt{MSSF} ($w \in \{1,3,5\} \le H$), and \texttt{BTO-SRSS} spectral subspace weighting \citep{ledoit2004shrinkage} with counterfactual leave-one-out \texttt{Shapley-diffu-GRPO} and a friction-dominance basis filter ($\text{IC}_j - 0.32\,\text{Turn}_j > -0.005$).
  \item \textbf{Chamber 1B (G\^arleanu--Pedersen FWL Ridge Backbone)}: Fits a sample-size-scaled KKT non-negative Ridge regressor ($\alpha_{\text{reg}} = 0.15\,N_{\text{valid}}$, $\text{positive=True}$) strictly on $\mathcal{T}_{\text{search}}$ across 7 strictly causal economic views (PIT value-quality, ROE--P/B rank spread, smoothed 15d risk-adjusted momentum, 3d-skipped 12d momentum, 3d volume-shock overheat reversal, 5d and 10d foreign flow), damping partial betas by $(\tau_0 + \text{Turn}_j)^{-0.50}$ \citep{garleanu2013dynamic}. Chambers 1A and 1B are fused causally via 30-day lagged reflection adaptation around search-CV prior $\alpha_{\text{sym}}$.
  \item \textbf{Chamber 2 (Devil's Advocate Pre-Mortem Auditor)}: Before ranking, computes causal stock-level hazard penalties $p^{\text{PM}}_{i,t}$ flagging repeated floor-lock proximity, parabolic 3d volume blow-off traps under T+2.5 supply risk, unfillable ceiling locks, intraday volatility spikes, and deep distress drift ($r_{20d} < -24\%$).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Module 3: Scale-Invariant \texttt{Hierarchical Regime-Conviction Controller}}
Layer 1 (cross-sectional alpha ranking) is decoupled from Layer 2 (risk and execution discipline) using scale-invariant regime boundaries (\texttt{vol\_ratio} instead of hardcoded absolute volatility thresholds, ensuring cross-market portability across VN, US, and CSI):
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{Bull Conviction} ($r_{20d} > 2\%$, breadth $\ge 55\%$, $\text{vol\_ratio} < 1.25$): relaxes BAB drag ($0.30\,\lambda_{\text{BAB}}$) to capture bull leaders.
  \item \textbf{Cautious Defense} ($\text{vol\_ratio} > 1.35$, $\text{DD}_{60} < -8.5\%$, or trauma score $> 0.44$): escalates Vasicek-shrunk Frazzini--Pedersen BAB + quality defensive tilting ($\lambda_{\text{BAB}} + \lambda_{\text{stress}} \cdot \text{stress}_t$) to rotate seamlessly into low-beta, low-volatility high-quality holdings without paying 70\,bps cash-exit whipsaw.
  \item \textbf{Zero-Whipsaw Instant-Release Tail Circuit Breaker}: retreats to \texttt{Cash\_Shield} (all-$\text{NaN}$) \emph{only} under simultaneous deep drawdown ($\text{DD}_{60} \le \delta_{\text{dd}}$) and active multi-stock floor-lock contagion, releasing immediately once daily floor contagion subsides or a 5d rebound initiates ($r_{5d} \ge 1.5\%$), eliminating V-bottom lock-out.
  \item \textbf{No-Trade-Band Incumbent Hysteresis}: adds $h \in [0.20, 0.26]$ to current Top-$K$ incumbents $\mathcal{H}_{t-1}$, slashing borderline 6th/7th rank churn under Vietnam's T+2.5 sell tax.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Module 4: \texttt{Zero-Validation-Burn Purged Search CV} \& 10-Year Veteran Reasoning Engine}
All 62 search candidates, \texttt{MSSF} windows, \texttt{BTO-SRSS} weights, and all 4 controller profiles are scored exclusively inside a hermetically isolated search-zone purged walk-forward simulator (\texttt{self.local\_search\_sim}), emitting exactly $M_{\text{val}} = 1$ champion to \texttt{SealedEvaluator}. Complementing quantitative signals, our 10-year veteran reasoning module (\texttt{src/vn\_rsi/veteran/}) mines 90 strictly search-zone episodes ($\le \text{2022-12-30}$) across 6 regimes/traps to construct 5-step CIO SFT trajectories and Veteran-vs-FOMO DPO preference pairs \citep{rafailov2023dpo}.

\subsection{100\% Free-Tier Cloud Training Architecture ($<4.5$\,GB Peak VRAM)}
Both \texttt{Tree-MDLM} and \texttt{Holo-HRM} scale across five hardware presets (\texttt{cloud\_presets.py}) with explicit inter-stage \texttt{gc.collect()} + \texttt{torch.cuda.empty\_cache()} cleanup and automatic \texttt{--resume} checkpointing across 12-hour free sessions. We explicitly distinguish \emph{directly measured local CPU telemetry} from \emph{analytical free-T4 VRAM budgets}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{Local CPU / CI (\texttt{cpu\_fast}, Directly Measured)}: $96.5$K structural parameters ($\sim 85$\,MB RAM), complete 6-round arena run in $87.6$\,s (2-round smoke run in $33.63$\,s); Stage-1 \texttt{--resume} reloads in $0.02$\,s.
  \item \textbf{Google Colab Free (1$\times$ T4 16\,GB, \texttt{kaggle\_t4\_optimal}, Analytical NF4 Budget)}: $\sim 10$M structural parameters ($<0.25$\,GB VRAM) followed sequentially by 4-bit NF4 QLoRA SFT + reference-free PEFT DPO \citep{dettmers2023qlora,rafailov2023dpo} on \texttt{Qwen3-1.7B} \citep{yang2025qwen3} ($\approx 3.4$\,GB peak VRAM) or \texttt{Qwen3-4B} ($\approx 4.3$\,GB peak VRAM, strictly $<4.5$\,GB).
  \item \textbf{Kaggle Free (T4$\times$2 32\,GB / TPU v5e-8 128\,GB HBM) \& Modal Free Serverless}: scales cleanly to \texttt{kaggle\_max\_100m} ($\sim 158$M FP16) and \texttt{tpu\_v5e\_350m} / \texttt{cloud\_a100\_350m} ($\sim 357$M BF16).

\begin{proof}[Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:homogeneity}]
We proceed by structural induction on the AST $\phi$. Let $\sigma_{\lambda}(P, V) = (\lambda_P P, \lambda_V V)$ denote positive price/volume rescaling with $\lambda_P, \lambda_V > 0$.
\emph{Base case:} Every terminal $x \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{term}}$ satisfies $x(\sigma_\lambda(\mathcal{F}_t)) = \lambda_P^{p_x} \lambda_V^{v_x} x(\mathcal{F}_t)$ by construction (e.g., \texttt{close}, \texttt{open}, \texttt{high}, \texttt{low} carry $(1,0)$; \texttt{volume} carries $(0,1)$; \texttt{turnover\_vnd} carries $(1,1)$; returns, ratios, PIT ROE/P/B, and limit distances carry $(0,0)$).
\emph{Inductive step:} Suppose subtrees $a, b$ are homogeneous of degrees $d(a)=(p_a, v_a)$ and $d(b)=(p_b, v_b)$.
(i)~For \texttt{add}/\texttt{sub}, \texttt{TypedASTGuard} enforces $d(a)=d(b)$, so $(a \pm b)(\sigma_\lambda) = \lambda_P^{p_a}\lambda_V^{v_a}(a \pm b)$.
(ii)~For \texttt{mul} and \texttt{safe\_div}, exponents add/subtract, yielding homogeneity degrees $(p_a+p_b, v_a+v_b)$ and $(p_a-p_b, v_a-v_b)$ (up to $\epsilon=10^{-8}$ numerical stabilization).
(iii)~For rolling \texttt{ts\_mean}/\texttt{ts\_std}/\texttt{ts\_delta}/\texttt{lag} over window $w \ge 1$, linearity and positive homogeneity preserve $(p_a, v_a)$ using exclusively lags $\{t-w+1,\dots,t\} \subseteq \mathcal{F}_t$.
(iv)~For \texttt{ts\_return}, \texttt{ts\_zscore}, \texttt{cs\_rank}, and \texttt{cs\_zscore}, scalar prefactors $\lambda_P^{p_a}\lambda_V^{v_a} > 0$ cancel identically in ratios and order statistics, mapping any dimension to $(0,0)$.
Hence every verified root $\phi$ with $d(\phi)=(0,0)$ satisfies $\phi(\sigma_\lambda(\mathcal{F}_t)) = \phi(\mathcal{F}_t)$ and is $\mathcal{F}_t$-measurable.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:dsr_bound}]
Under \citet{bailey2014deflated}, $\text{DSR}(\widehat{\text{SR}}; M) = \Phi\!\left(\frac{(\widehat{\text{SR}} - \text{SR}_0(M))\sqrt{T_{\text{eval}}-1}}{\sqrt{1 - \hat{\gamma}_3 \widehat{\text{SR}} + \frac{\hat{\gamma}_4 - 1}{4}\widehat{\text{SR}}^2}}\right)$, where $\Phi$ is the standard normal CDF. Because $\Phi^{-1}(1 - 1/M)$ and $\Phi^{-1}(1 - 1/(Me))$ are strictly increasing in $M \ge 2$ and $\text{SR}_0(1)=0 < \text{SR}_0(2) < \dots < \text{SR}_0(M)$, isolating all $K_{\text{inner}}$ screening evaluations inside $\mathcal{T}_{\text{search}}$ (disjoint from $\mathcal{T}_{\text{val}}$ and $\mathcal{T}_{\text{test}}$) strictly reduces official validation/test trials from $M_{\text{legacy}} = K_{\text{inner}} + M_{\text{val}} \gg 1$ (e.g., $81$ in Holo-MAVE-Q v3) to $M_{\text{v4}} = 1$ (validation) and $2$ (validation + single-shot sealed test). Consequently $\text{SR}_0(M_{\text{v4}}) < \text{SR}_0(M_{\text{legacy}})$, and monotonicity of $\Phi$ guarantees $\text{DSR}(\widehat{\text{SR}}; M_{\text{v4}}) > \text{DSR}(\widehat{\text{SR}}; M_{\text{legacy}})$ for any $\widehat{\text{SR}} > 0$ with positive denominator.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:bab_friction}]
Consider a one-period mean-variance-friction objective over weight vector $w_t \in \Delta_K = \{w \ge 0 : \mathbf{1}^\top w = 1, w_i \in \{0, 1/K\}\}$ under a single-index covariance model $\Sigma_t = \sigma_{m,t}^2 \beta_t \beta_t^\top + \sigma_\varepsilon^2 I$ and proportional rebalance cost $\bar{c}\|w_t - w_{t-1}\|_1$:
\[
  \max_{w_t \in \Delta_K} \; w_t^\top \hat{\alpha}_t - \frac{\gamma_{\text{risk}}}{2} w_t^\top \Sigma_t w_t - \bar{c} \|w_t - w_{t-1}\|_1 - \eta_{\text{PM}} w_t^\top p^{\text{PM}}_t.
\]
Expanding the quadratic term around the average portfolio beta $\bar{\beta}_t = w_t^\top \beta_t$ yields marginal risk contribution $\frac{\partial}{\partial w_{i,t}}\left(\frac{\gamma_{\text{risk}}}{2} \sigma_{m,t}^2 (w_t^\top \beta_t)^2\right) = \gamma_{\text{risk}} \sigma_{m,t}^2 \bar{\beta}_t \beta_{i,t} =: \lambda_{\text{BAB}}(\mathcal{R}_t)\,\beta_{i,t}$, which increases monotonically with market variance $\sigma_{m,t}^2$ (regime stress $\mathcal{R}_t$). Replacing noisy OLS betas with Vasicek Bayesian shrinkage $\tilde{\beta}^{\text{Vas}}_{i,t} = 0.65 \hat{\beta}_{i,t} + 0.35$ minimizes mean-squared estimation error \citep{vasicek1973note}. Meanwhile, the $\ell_1$ turnover penalty $-\bar{c}\|w_t - w_{t-1}\|_1$ contributes a marginal holding bonus $+2\bar{c}$ for every incumbent $i \in \mathcal{H}_{t-1}$ (selecting an incumbent avoids both a sell cost on $i$ and a buy cost on its replacement), directly motivating the no-trade-band hysteresis term $+h\,\mathbb{I}[i \in \mathcal{H}_{t-1}]$. Finally, following \citet{garleanu2013dynamic}, discounting each alpha predictor $j$ with decay/turnover rate $\text{Turn}_j$ by $(\tau_0 + \text{Turn}_j)^{-1/2}$ aligns predictor weights with the square-root transaction-cost aim portfolio. Combining these four marginal terms yields Equation~\eqref{eq:cio_score}.
\end{proof}
